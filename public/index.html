About

Puzzle Inventory, version SEHv1.06, made by Tom.

It keeps track of:

  • Every puzzle, with photos, ratings and where it's stored
  • Each completion, with the date, time taken and notes
  • A quality score for the puzzles worth judging
  • A wishlist of puzzles to buy

Everything is saved on the home server in the app's data and uploads folders.
