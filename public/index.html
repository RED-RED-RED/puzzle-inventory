About Puzzle Inventory

Puzzle Inventory Manager

Version: SEHv1.02

Created by: Tom

Features:

  • Track your entire puzzle collection
  • Log completion times and notes
  • Upload and manage puzzle images
  • Rate puzzle quality with detailed assessment
  • Organize with brands, themes, and custom fields
  • Maintain a wishlist of puzzles to buy
  • Full server-side data storage
  • Customizable color themes

All data stored server-side in:

  • /data/puzzles.json - Puzzle data
  • /data/settings.json - Settings & preferences
  • /data/wishlist.json - Wishlist items
  • /data/theme.json - Color theme
  • /uploads/puzzles/ - Puzzle images
